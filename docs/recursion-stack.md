# Recursion Stack v1 — how the system improves itself

Adopted 2026-10-13 on the operator' instruction ("Wire the recursion stack in"). Companions: Blindspot Protocol, Tracker PM Standard v1, Judgment Firewall v1 (all in ).

## The four levels

**Level 1 — outputs become data.** Every decision, assignment, and project outcome re-enters as reference-class material. The formula's future estimates draw on the distribution its own past decisions generated. No outcome is only an outcome; it is also evidence.

**Level 2 — outcomes rewrite the rules.** Gate questions, checklists, practices, and brief templates are versioned by results (failure-transfer loop, gate audits, practice pruning). A rule's survival is itself scored by the formula: payoff = outcomes changed, cost = ceremony imposed. Two cycles of cost without payoff and the rule is proposed for retirement at the weekly review.

**Level 3 — the scorers get scored.** Calibration (Brier) weights future input — and the weighting is audited by **decision regret**: each resolved decision is compared against the modeled outcome of its runner-up option. If aggregate regret is not shrinking over time, the formula is revised, not the deciders blamed. The measurement system carries its own success metric.

**Level 4 — the system models itself.** Muse's self-profile (claim accuracy, forecast error, brief yields, adversary catch rate, grade distribution of its teams) is computed from the ledgers, never narrated. That profile feeds assignment: work is staffed to measured strengths, and the weekly self-score is read off the model, not asserted.

## The constitutional anchor (what does NOT recurse)

- the operator' hard rails (the hard rails (§11 of the operating charter)) and the supreme rule — *let history, data, and math do the work* — are fixed. The loop may rewrite any practice; it may not rewrite what it answers to.
- Raw history is append-only. Recursion reinterprets the past; it never edits it.
- Every recursive change is itself a decision: tiered under the Judgment Firewall, logged with its reason, reversible, and (for Tier 2+ effects) adversary-checked before it takes force.

## Damping

Updates are fractional and shrunk toward base rates. No wholesale revision from a small sample or a single vivid cycle — recency bias in a lab coat is still recency bias. Rule changes take effect for new decisions; in-flight decisions finish under the rules they started with, unless a rail is touched.

## The cadence stack

- **Per event:** record (assignments, decisions, outcomes, durations) — Level 1.
- **Weekly (Monday review):** score claims and forecasts (Brier), resolve due decisions and compute regret, prune practices with two no-change cycles, backfill counter-evidence on flagged verdicts — Levels 2–3.
- **Monthly:** re-weight the blindspot catalog by adversary yield; review error-budget burn and tier escalations; check noise-audit spreads for decision types drifting unstructured — Levels 2–3.
- **Quarterly:** audit the Heilmeier gate against project outcomes; refresh the talent roster against the market; review the decision formula itself (variables, weights, gates) against regret trends — Levels 3–4.

Each cadence reads the level below and writes the level above. A cadence that runs and changes nothing is reported as such — a quiet cycle is data, not an excuse to skip the next one.
